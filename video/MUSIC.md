# Music for the year-in-review video

Background beds, matched to the genres in each part of the video. Each one is saved as
`video/public/music/<style>.mp3` (gitignored: Pixabay's license doesn't allow sharing the files themselves).
A missing file just leaves that part without music.

All tracks are from Pixabay under the [Pixabay Content License](https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/):
free to use in videos, including commercially, with no credit required. Credit is given here anyway.
